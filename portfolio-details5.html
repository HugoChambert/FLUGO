COINVOY: The Entrepreneur-Focused Global Crypto Exchange

COINVOY is a sleek, fast, and secure crypto exchange operating in France, Thailand, and the United States, built specifically for entrepreneurs and global innovators.

With ultra-low processing fees, COINVOY gives users more value than many major exchanges—making trading, payments, and international transactions simpler and more affordable.

  • Multi-region trading across France, Thailand, and the United States
  • Low processing fees designed for entrepreneurs
  • Enterprise-level security for safe transactions
  • Built for business workflows and future integration with RECONTRE

COINVOY is designed to evolve, with future plans to integrate directly with your other project, RECONTRE, creating a powerful ecosystem for networking, collaboration, and borderless digital finance.
