P[ART]AKING: Fostering Collaboration in Artistic and Design Practices

P[ART]AKING emerged as a personal passion project during the summer of 2022, driven by my aspiration to cultivate a collaborative environment where artists and designers could converge to exchange innovative ideas and nurture one another's creative journeys. This initiative aimed to create a dynamic space that fosters dialogue and inspiration among creative practitioners.

Among the various designs developed throughout this period, "N°1" stands out as a significant representation of this endeavor. It encapsulates the ongoing work and refinements characteristic of my commitment to collaboration and creative exploration. This project not only reflects my artistic vision but also serves as a testament to the power of collective creativity in driving artistic innovation.
